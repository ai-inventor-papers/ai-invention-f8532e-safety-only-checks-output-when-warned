## 1 Introduction

Refusal in chat models is mediated by a single direction in the residual stream (Arditi et al., 2024). This mediation unfolds across layers in three stages: input-side harmfulness detection, aggregation along the refusal direction, and late-layer expression (Lee et al., 2025). Lee et al. identify content-axis features upstream of refusal—what the prompt is about—leaving the late-layer expression stage largely unexamined.
