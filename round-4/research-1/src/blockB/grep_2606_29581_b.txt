### 3.4 Safety Judges

We employ a six-judge safety ensemble to ensure verdict robustness across model families, generations, and architectural lineages. Judges run in parallel across both GPUs on the same generation files. Final ensemble verdicts use majority vote; individual judge verdicts are retained for agreement analysis.

## 5 Discussion

#### Standard quantization largely preserves safety.

Across 9 models and two quantization methods, AWQ INT4 maintains or reduces ASR relative to FP16 for 7 of 9 models. The effect is strongest among well-aligned models: Llama-3.2-3B, Qwen3-4B, Qwen3-8B, and OLMo-2-7B remain at 0% ASR across all precisions, while Llama-3.1-8B and the Granite variants stay near the floor.
