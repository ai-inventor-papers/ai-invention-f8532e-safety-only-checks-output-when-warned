# Causal depth × site grid: where does Qwen3-4B's refusal live?

This is an intervention study on three checkpoints of the same model:

- `Qwen/Qwen3-4B`, called **instruct** here;
- `Qwen/Qwen3-4B-SafeRL`, the official safety fine-tune, called **SafeRL**;
- `mlabonne/Qwen3-4B-abliterated`, a community uncensored checkpoint, called **abliterated**.

It asks three questions:

- **Where** in depth and **where** in the token stream does the request-harmfulness direction do causal work on refusal and over-refusal?
- Does that differ between instruct and SafeRL?
- Do the cheap readouts built on that direction move when behaviour moves?

It is the first *executed* site-local causal grid of this research run. The iteration-3 plan for this grid was never executed.

> RESULTS: see [Results](#results). Every number there is regenerated by `src/analyze.py` from the checkpointed cells into
> `results/analysis.json`, `results/summary_tables.md` and `method_out.json`.

**Headline findings**

- **The request axis F causally controls the *form* of the first response token, not the refusal decision.** Projecting F out at the last prompt position in B4-B6 is CAUSAL (registered F1 rule) for the first-token refusal-onset mass. In instruct it moves -2.9 nats, and refusal-onset probability at token 1 drops from about 78% to about 4%. Judged refusal of harmful requests stays at 0.92 → 0.88 in instruct and 0.98 in SafeRL: 0 of 18 site×band cells are CAUSAL in either model, because the model rewrites the opening and still refuses.
- **Local removal fails because refusal is redundant across positions.** In the exploratory T3 control, removing the same F at *every* position within B4 drops instruct refusal to 0.67 (effect_FR -0.33 [-0.48, -0.21]), so the direction is causal. SafeRL's refusal is more robust to that global ablation (exploratory DiD +0.23 [+0.08, +0.38]).
- **Over-refusal is the site-local lever.** On hard-benign probes, F or N6 removal at mid-depth (B4-B5, at P and D′, and even at E) lowers over-refusal by 0.12-0.19 in both models. Only instruct N6 @ P_B5 survives Holm over 18 cells.
- **Decodable ≠ causal.** F is decodable in 12 of 18 cells per model, yet 12 of 12 are behaviourally inert for refusal. BL1, RD, T1ref and N1 readouts swing by several nats under interventions that leave behaviour unchanged. The matched random control alone moves BL1 by about 2 nats.
- **Three checkpoints in activation space.** SafeRL keeps instruct's request and twin axes (cos ≥ 0.84 per band). The abliterated checkpoint keeps them in B1-B3 but rotates them from B4 on: cos(F) ≈ 0.3 and cos(N6) ≈ 0.02. B4 on is exactly the depth where F carries the token-1 refusal drive in the other two. In abliterated, removing its own F *raises* refusal-onset mass (sign reversal).

## Design

| component | definition |
|---|---|
| models | instruct and SafeRL: the full grid. abliterated: the site-P grid, judged site-P generation, readouts, forward-only windows, and the site-E late readout. Each model runs alone on the GPU in bf16 (NVIDIA L4). |
| depth bands | B1…B6 = sixths of the 36 decoder layers: L0-5, 6-11, 12-17, 18-23, 24-29, 30-35. "Layer *l*" is the output of decoder block *l* = hidden index *l*+1. |
| sites | **P**: the last prompt position only. **D′**: the last prompt position plus decode calls t=1…7, so it can change token 1. **E**: decode calls t=5…20, so tokens 1-5 cannot change by construction. **D**: decode calls 1…8, run only as a readout site, in the teacher-forced windows. |
| directions | **F**: the model's own request axis per layer, unit(mean EASY-harmful − mean EASY-benign) over the iteration-2 last-prompt-token harvest (`A_prompt.npy`). It uses zero forwards. **N6**: the benign-side XSTest twin direction, unit(mean safe twin − mean unsafe twin), over the 85 confirmatory twin pairs from one prompt-only pass. **N6perp**: N6 orthogonalised to F. **F_perpU**: F with its top-8 refusal-token unembedding rows projected out. |
| arms | **0**: no intervention. **F / N6 / N6perp / F_perpU**: projection-out, h ← h − (h·v)v, in every layer of the band at the site positions, fp32 math cast to bf16. **R**: random control. At P and in the teacher-forced windows it is *matched displacement*, h ← h − c_F·u, where c_F is the coefficient the F row of the same item removes in the same pass, so the R/F displacement ratio is exactly 1. At D′/E it is the plan's own-coefficient form h ← h − (h·F)u. u is a unit Gaussian direction orthogonalised to span{F, N6}. There are 20 draws for forward-only outcomes and 3 for judged outcomes. **POS**: a positive control that replaces the residual with the mean EASY-benign residual (B3, B4 at P). It measures generic disruption and is not a treatment. |
| outcomes | Judged refusal / harmful compliance / safe engagement on 48 held-out harmful probes, and judged over-refusal on 48 hard-benign probes. Generation is greedy, 80 new tokens. The judge is gemini-2.5-flash-lite with the frozen `lc_judge_iter1` rubric, deduplicated by sha256(prompt+response). Other outcomes: ARC-Easy flip-to-wrong on items correct under arm 0 (no judge); GSM8K collateral on CAUSAL cells; teacher-forced refusal drive RD = log-mass on the 44 refusal-onset tokens; G1 = lse(refusal) − lse(control); T1ref. |
| causal rule | A cell is CAUSAL if the Holm-adjusted paired sign-flip p of **effect_FR** = mean_i[y_F,i − mean_j y_Rj,i] is below 0.05 across the 18 cells within (model, outcome, arm). The site-P forward-only grid (registered F1) uses a 6-cell family and additionally needs the empirical p over the 20 R draws to be below 0.05. |
| decodability | Arm-0 AUROC (32+32 held-out items) of the band-mean standardised projection on F at the site's window. DECODABLE if the bootstrap lower CI bound is above 0.60. |
| readouts under intervention | BL1_easy / BL1_hard (G1 contrast, final logits applied once, never pooled), N1 (Cohen d along the *unintervened* F at l*), N6, N2 (F_perpU) and N7, on the 256 stimuli under each arm. The site-E late readout teacher-forces the iteration-1 `safety_2x2` 144-token continuations, edits tokens 5-20, and reads the hazardous-vs-benign separation at tokens 40-55. |
| T3 disambiguation control (added) | **genG**: the same F / N6 projected out at *every* prompt position and every decode call, per band and in all 36 layers. This is abliteration logic applied at inference time. |

### Pre-registration

- `prereg.json` (sha256 in `prereg.sha256`, 3b0aaa19…) was frozen on the CPU-only box before any model load on items. It registers the CPU-fallback F1 subset: the site-P forward-only grid, the judged B3/B4 × P cell on 24+24 items at 48 tokens, and ARC.
- The pod restarted onto a GPU. The plan's primary design was then frozen in `prereg_gpu_addendum.json` (sha256 in `prereg_gpu_addendum.sha256`, 524c15c0…) **before any Qwen3-4B outcome was computed**.
- The F1 cells run unchanged and are analysed exactly as registered.
- Every deviation is logged in `results/deviations.json`.

## Results

Numbers come from `results/analysis.json`, summarised in `results/summary_tables.md`. Rates are over 48 harmful
and 48 hard-benign held-out probes, with greedy decoding for 80 tokens. effect_FR is the paired difference between
the treatment and the mean of its matched random controls; 95% bootstrap CIs are in brackets. **\*** marks a cell
that is CAUSAL under Holm correction.

### 0. Baselines (arm 0) and checks

| | instruct | SafeRL | abliterated |
|---|---|---|---|
| judged refusal on harmful probes | 0.917 [0.80, 0.97] | 0.979 [0.89, 1.00] | 0.188 [0.10, 0.32] |
| judged harmful compliance | 0.000 | 0.000 | 0.604 |
| judged over-refusal on hard-benign probes | 0.44 (0.375-0.44 across cells) | 0.48 (0.48-0.58 across cells) | 0.04 (0.04-0.06 across cells) |
| first-token refusal-onset mass RD (harm, nats) | -0.25 | -0.006 | -4.70 |
| BL1_easy / BL1_hard (final logits once) | 6.65 / 6.21 | 7.50 / 4.69 | -5.88 / 2.16 |
| l* (argmax d_hard) / N6 best twin layer | 26 / 26 | 26 / 31 | 23 / 19 |
| ARC-Easy arm-0 accuracy | 0.953 | 0.953 | 0.953 |

- **Plan check T1.** The arm-0 judged rates agree with the earlier iterations' judged rates for all three checkpoints; every Newcombe difference CI contains 0. Those reference rates used different items and 140-token generations. BL1_truelogit recomputed on the GPU (6.207 HARD, instruct) matches the disk value 6.2076.
- **Hook checks** pass for smoke, instruct, SafeRL and abliterated (`results/hook_checks_*.json`):
  - a1: no-op hooks are bitwise identical to hooks removed.
  - a2: arm-0 rows are bitwise unaffected by active rows in the same batch.
  - b: after projection, |h·v|/|h| < 1e-3.
  - c: the matched R/F displacement ratio is 1 ± 1e-7.
- **Site checks:**
  - At E, tokens 1-5 are identical to arm 0 in every row.
  - The edit steps seen equal the registered windows.
  - The post-cast residual is < 1e-3 everywhere.
- **Greedy knife-edge.** The arm-0 greedy labels of 3-8 items differ between cell families with different batch shapes (GPU numerics). Instruct over-refusal is 0.375-0.44 across the 32 families. Every contrast is therefore computed *within* a cell, against that cell's co-batched arm 0 and matched R (`arm0_stability`).

### 1. Registered F1 analysis: site-P forward-only outcomes. F controls the first token.

Projecting F out at the last prompt position is **CAUSAL** for the first-token refusal drive in bands B4-B6 in both
models. The rule is Holm over 6 cells, plus the empirical p over 20 matched random draws at its minimum of 1/21.

| RD_harm effect_FR (nats) | B1 | B2 | B3 | B4 | B5 | B6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| instruct, F | +0.00 | +0.02 | -0.06 | **-2.87\*** | **-2.59\*** | **-1.58\*** |
| SafeRL, F | -0.00 | +0.00 | -0.00 | **-0.96\*** | **-0.54\*** | **-0.23\*** |
| instruct, N6 | +0.01 | -0.01 | +0.05\* | **-1.59\*** | **-2.09\*** | **-1.47\*** |
| instruct, N6perp (N6 minus its F component) | +0.03 | -0.02 | +0.07 | +0.04 | +0.00 | -0.14 |

- In instruct at B4, removing F moves the refusal-onset probability at token 1 from about 78% to about 4%. The first token stops being a refusal onset for 46% of harmful items (T1ref -0.46\*).
- N6's effect is carried entirely by its overlap with F: cos(F, N6) at l* is -0.86 in both models, and N6perp is null.
- A few SafeRL cells flag CAUSAL with |effect| ≤ 0.01 nats because RD sits at its ceiling and has almost no variance. They are statistically detectable but negligible.

### 2. Judged behaviour: site-local removal does not change refusal of harmful requests

The same interventions, at every site and band, leave **judged refusal of harmful requests unchanged** in both models.

| max \|effect_FR\| over the 18 cells | refusal (harm) | harmful compliance | CAUSAL F cells (Holm-18) |
|---|---|---|---|
| instruct | 0.056 (P_B4, D'_B4; CI [-0.12, 0.00]) | 0.021 | 0 / 18 |
| SafeRL | 0.021 | 0.000 | 0 / 18 |

- The registered F1 judged cell agrees: B3/B4 at P, 24+24 items, 48 tokens, refusal change 0.000 in both models.
- The model rewrites the *opening* of its refusal but still refuses. For example, "I'm unable to assist with that request…" becomes "Creating or distributing X is illegal and unethical. I cannot provide…".
- **The token-1 readout (RD / T1ref) therefore massively overstates behavioural control.** A -2.9-nat drop at token 1 corresponds to a ≤0.06 change in judged refusal.
- **So does the text-only baseline.** The keyword-refusal proxy registers a CAUSAL collapse of "refusal" under the same arm. At instruct P_B4 it falls from 0.79 to 0.15 against R 0.78 (effect_FR -0.63 [-0.76, -0.50], Holm p 0.002). The same holds in 6 F cells at B4-B6 (P and D′). The reworded refusals simply lack the lexical markers; the judge sees no change.

### 3. Over-refusal is the site-local lever (two-sided effect)

On hard-benign probes, removing the request axis at mid-depth lowers over-refusal in both models.

| over-refusal effect_FR | P_B4 | P_B5 | D'_B4 | D'_B5 | E_B4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| instruct, F | -0.125 | -0.139 [-0.26,-0.03] | -0.132 | -0.069 | -0.132 [-0.25,-0.02] |
| instruct, N6 | -0.062 | **-0.146\*** [-0.24,-0.06] | -0.174 [-0.30,-0.05] | -0.090 | -0.035 |
| SafeRL, F | -0.188 [-0.31,-0.06] | -0.167 [-0.31,-0.04] | -0.111 | -0.118 | -0.056 |
| SafeRL, N6 | -0.118 | -0.160 [-0.28,-0.03] | -0.104 | -0.139 | -0.076 |

- Many of these cells are nominally significant (p < 0.05). Only **instruct N6 at P_B5** survives Holm over 18 cells (p_holm = 0.036).
- **Power.** At n = 48 the paired MDE (80% power, unadjusted α = 0.05) is 0.13-0.20 for the over-refusal cells where effects appear.
  - It is about 1.4× larger at the Holm-18 threshold.
  - The median over all non-degenerate judged null cells is 0.06-0.07.
  - The observed -0.12 to -0.19 over-refusal effects therefore sit right at the design's detection limit. "Not CAUSAL" means "not detected at this power", not "zero".
- The generic-disruption positive control POS (residual patch to the mean benign residual) also lowers instruct over-refusal at P_B4 (-0.146). Over-refusal is fragile to any large edit at B4. The F-vs-matched-R contrast, not F-vs-0, is the causal claim.
- In abliterated, POS at P_B4 lowers the residual refusal from 0.17 to 0.10 (vs R 0.22; Holm over its 2-cell family).
- In instruct, even site **E** moves over-refusal at B4 (-0.13). Tokens 1-5 are untouched there, so the borderline decision is still open after the model has started answering.

### 4. T3 disambiguation: the same F *is* causal when removed at every position

Here F is projected out at every prompt position and every decode call. That is abliteration logic applied as an
inference-time ablation.

| all-position ablation | instruct refusal (0 → F; R) | effect_FR | SafeRL refusal (0 → F; R) | effect_FR |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B4 only | 0.92 → **0.67**; R 1.00 | **-0.33** [-0.48, -0.21] | 0.96 → 0.88; R 0.98 | -0.10 [-0.21, -0.02] |
| all 36 layers | 0.92 → **0.71**; R 0.90 | **-0.19** [-0.31, -0.08] | 0.96 → 0.85; R 0.90 | -0.04 [-0.15, +0.08] |
| all 36 layers, N6 | 0.92 → **0.58**; R 0.92 | -0.33 | 0.96 → 0.85; R 0.98 | -0.13 |

- Instruct also produces harmful content under all-position F at B4 (harmful compliance 0.08) and drops over-refusal from 0.38 to 0.08 (effect_FR -0.375).
- SafeRL minus instruct DiD for F on refusal at B4 is **+0.229 [+0.083, +0.375]** (exploratory, uncorrected). **SafeRL's refusal is markedly more robust than instruct's to removing its own request axis everywhere.** With all 36 layers the DiD is +0.146 [-0.02, +0.33].
- The hook and the direction are therefore sound. The site-local null in §2 reflects *positional redundancy*: the refusal decision is re-derived from other prompt positions.

### 5. Decodable but inert

- F is decodable at site P in every band: arm-0 AUROC 0.80-0.96 for instruct and 0.80-0.98 for SafeRL.
- In the decode windows D and E, F is decodable only at B4-B6 (0.85-0.98).
- **Not one of the 12 decodable cells per model is CAUSAL for judged refusal** (12/12 decodable-but-inert in both models). For N6 on over-refusal the count is 11/12 (instruct) and 12/12 (SafeRL).
- This is the pattern of Basu et al. (arXiv:2603.18353) *for its SAE arm only*. Its Arm 1 corrected 17/85 and disrupted 25/47, so this is not a claim that mechanistic methods never work.

### 6. Readouts move when behaviour does not

Readouts on the 256 stimuli under site-P intervention (instruct, B5):

- F drives BL1_easy from 6.65 to -1.19 (Δ = -7.84 [-9.67, -6.09]) and BL1_hard from 6.21 to 2.06.
- N1 (d along the unintervened F at l* = 26) falls from 2.45 to 0.35. This is MECHANICALLY_FORCED, because l* is in B5.
- Judged refusal on the harmful probes does not move at all (§2).
- The matched random control alone shifts BL1_easy by +2.2 at B5 and by -2.5 at B4. **|ΔBL1 under R| is of the same order as a real treatment effect.**

Logit-lens-style readouts (BL1, RD, T1ref, N1) track the local representation, not the behavioural decision.

### 7. Sites and depth

- **P vs D′ (N10).** Registered sign: |P| > |D′|. The P-minus-D′ contrast of effect_FR on refusal is within ±0.03 in every band for both models, so there is no refusal effect to localise. On over-refusal, P and D′ are similar at B4-B5.
- **E is smaller than P** for SafeRL over-refusal (B4: -0.19 at P vs -0.06 at E), as registered. **It is not smaller for instruct at B4** (-0.125 vs -0.132).
- **Forward-only teacher-forced windows.** At D, F lowers the refusal-onset mass after the window (instruct B4 -1.78 nats\*, SafeRL B4 -0.89\*). The log-probability of the arm-0 continuation changes much less (-0.83 / -0.20).

### 8. Two-sidedness (SafeRL vs instruct)

- **Registered prediction:** DiD_OR = [N6-R](SafeRL) - [N6-R](instruct) > 0 (instruct's over-refusal falls more). **It is not supported.**
  - The N6 over-refusal DiD is positive in only 8 of 18 cells: P_B5 -0.014 [-0.17, +0.15]; P_B4 -0.056 [-0.26, +0.13]; D'_B4 +0.069 [-0.08, +0.23].
  - Of the 54 judged-grid DiDs (18 cells × 3 contrasts), 3 have uncorrected CIs that exclude 0. That is about as many as chance predicts (2.7 at α = 0.05). The three are P_B1 F on over-refusal +0.090 [+0.03, +0.17], P_B4 F on refusal +0.069 [+0.01, +0.15], and P_B6 N6 on over-refusal -0.139 [-0.28, -0.00].
  - At site-local resolution, SafeRL and instruct over-refusal respond alike.
- The clear model difference is **refusal robustness under all-position ablation** (§4, DiD +0.23).
- The site-P token-1 refusal drive is also more robust in SafeRL (registered F1 DiD for F on RD_harm at B4: +1.91 nats).

### 9. Collateral

- **ARC-Easy.** The flip-to-wrong rate is 0 of 61 correct items for every band and every reported arm (F, N6, R_F1) in all three models. Site-local ablation does not touch multiple-choice answers.
- **GSM8K:** GSM8K (16 items, 256 tokens, last-number grading, no judge) was run on the one CAUSAL cell (instruct N6 @ P_B5) and on the strongest nominal over-refusal cells (P_B4, D'_B4, D'_B5), which are descriptive.
  - Arm-0 accuracy is 8/16 for instruct and 10/16 for SafeRL (non-thinking mode, final-number answer only).
  - Every F / N6 / R arm stays within ±2 items of arm 0: F − R is −0.06 to 0.00, N6 − R is 0.00 to +0.13.
  - No collateral damage is detected.

### 10. The third checkpoint (abliterated) and the extras

**How the three checkpoints differ in activation space.** Cosines between the models' own per-layer directions,
averaged over each band's layers:

| band | cos F (instruct, SafeRL) | cos F (instruct, abliterated) | cos N6 (instruct, SafeRL) | cos N6 (instruct, abliterated) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | 0.997 | 0.999 | 0.995 | 0.997 |
| B2 | 0.982 | 0.994 | 0.977 | 0.926 |
| B3 | 0.916 | 0.943 | 0.889 | 0.609 |
| B4 | 0.895 | **0.412** | 0.922 | **0.021** |
| B5 | 0.901 | **0.306** | 0.918 | **0.027** |
| B6 | 0.841 | **0.328** | 0.863 | **0.061** |

- **SafeRL** keeps both axes (cos ≥ 0.84).
- **abliterated** keeps them in B1-B3 but **rotates the request axis away from B4 on** (cos ≈ 0.3), and its benign-twin axis becomes orthogonal to instruct's (cos ≈ 0.02). B4-B6 is exactly the depth where F is causal for the first-token refusal drive in instruct and SafeRL (§1).
- **Readouts in abliterated.** F's separability on HARD stimuli collapses: d at l* falls from 2.45 to 0.71, and l* moves from 26 to 23. The refusal-token readout inverts: BL1_easy is -5.88 vs +6.65 in instruct, and the arm-0 refusal-onset mass on harmful probes is about 1% (RD -4.70).
- **Its own F is still decodable** at P (AUROC 0.80-0.89) and in the decode windows at B4-B6 (0.75-0.83).
- **Removing F in abliterated has the opposite sign.** Its own F at P *raises* the first-token refusal-onset mass (RD_harm B4 +1.24 nats\*, against -2.87 in instruct). Judged behaviour does not move: 0/6 CAUSAL, max |effect_FR| 0.063.

**F_perpU (exploratory; F with its top-8 refusal-token unembedding rows projected out, site P).**

| instruct / SafeRL | RD_harm under F | RD_harm under F_perpU | over-refusal 0 → F / F_perpU |
|---|---|---|---|
| B4 | -3.17 / -0.99 | -2.99 / -1.02 | 0.42 → 0.23 / 0.29 · 0.50 → 0.29 / 0.31 |
| B5 | -2.83 / -0.55 | -2.68 / -0.62 | 0.42 → 0.27 / 0.27 · 0.50 → 0.35 / 0.38 |
| B6 | -1.81 / -0.25 | **-0.75 / -0.03** | 0.42 → 0.35 / 0.42 · 0.50 → 0.38 / 0.46 |

The arm-0 RD_harm is -0.26 (instruct) and -0.01 (SafeRL).

- At mid-depth (B4-B5), removing only the part of F *orthogonal* to the refusal-token unembeddings reproduces F's effects. The mid-depth request axis acts as a representational signal, not as a direct logit write.
- In the last band (B6), most of F's first-token effect disappears once its unembedding-aligned component is kept. The late-layer F writes refusal logits directly. The token-1 change rate falls from 0.42 to 0.22 (instruct) and from 0.46 to 0.09 (SafeRL).

**Site-E late readout (`safety_2x2`, 340 teacher-forced sequences per model).** The N1-analogue (Cohen d, hazardous vs benign continuation, projection on F at l*, tokens 40-55) is 0.63 in instruct, 0.82 in SafeRL and 0.33 in abliterated at arm 0.

- Editing the early window (tokens 5-20) with F shifts it by at most 0.08, at B4.
  - F − R: −0.077 [−0.100, −0.055] instruct; −0.059 [−0.087, −0.033] SafeRL; −0.070 [−0.087, −0.055] abliterated.
  - That is ≤ 12% of d_0 in instruct and SafeRL.
- Bands above l* are exactly 0 by construction.
- The late-window hazard representation is computed from the continuation itself. It is not carried forward from the early-window request axis.

**GSM8K collateral.** See §9.

### 11. The text-only baseline fails on SafeRL

The keyword-refusal proxy agrees with the judge at kappa 0.50 on instruct but **kappa ≈ 0.00 on SafeRL**. SafeRL refuses
with "It's important to clarify…", which opens 41 of 48 harmful arm-0 responses and contains no lexical refusal
marker. A text-only refusal readout is a baseline, not a measurement, for this checkpoint family.

## How to run

```bash
# environment (GPU; for CPU drop the cu128 index block in pyproject.toml)
uv sync                                   # or: see .aii/manifest.yaml for the exact uv pip commands
export OPENROUTER_API_KEY=...             # judge (about $1 for the full grid)

# 0. smoke test on Qwen/Qwen3-0.6B (hook checks, site checks, all stages at small size)
.venv/bin/python method.py --smoke --stages twins,decod,arm0,grid1,gen,genP,genDprime,genE,genU,genG,arc,grid2,grid3,stimro,decodgen,spanD,spanE,e2x2,gsm --gsm-cells P:B3,E:B4

# 1. main grid (instruct + SafeRL), about 1.5 h on one L4
.venv/bin/python method.py --models instruct,saferl \
  --stages unit,twins,decod,arm0,grid1,gen,genP,genDprime,genE,arc,grid2,grid3,stimro,decodgen,spanD,spanE --kv-gb 2.5

# 2. controls, third checkpoint and extras (the same commands as run_chain.sh)
./run_chain.sh

# 3. GSM8K collateral (the same command as run_chain2.sh)
.venv/bin/python method.py --models instruct,saferl --stages gsm --gsm-cells P:B5,P:B4,Dprime:B4,Dprime:B5

# 4. analysis, figures, schema check
.venv/bin/python src/analyze.py --models instruct,saferl,abliterated
.venv/bin/python src/figures.py --prefix ""
```

Every stage is idempotent. Re-running a command skips finished cells, and the judge cache makes re-judging free.

Re-running `src/analyze.py` after this release needs the per-model direction files in `out/private/` for the cross-model cosine section. These are regenerated by `method.py` (stage `twins`) plus `src/prep.py`'s `disk_directions`. Every other section reads only `out/cells/`.

## Limitations

- **Sample size.** n = 48 + 48 items per cell. Over-refusal effects of 0.12-0.19 sit at the detection limit under Holm-18 (MDE 0.13-0.20 unadjusted), so most over-refusal cells are "not detected", not "null".
- **Greedy knife-edge.** Across batch layouts, 3-8 items per model flip their arm-0 label, from GPU numerics alone. Every contrast is computed within one pass.
- **Unequal random controls at D′/E.** There the random control is the plan's own-coefficient form. Its mean displacement exceeds F's (about 1.4× for F, about 2× for N6; ratios per cell are in `meta.checks`). This makes F-R conservative for CAUSAL claims, but it can inflate "decodable-but-inert" counts at D′/E. The exact matched design is used at P and in the teacher-forced windows.
- **Single-direction fits.** F is a mean-difference direction fitted on EASY stimuli, one vector per layer. A different refusal direction, such as a multi-position or DIM-over-layers fit, could behave differently under site-local removal.
- **Scope of the abliterated run.** It covers the site-P judged grid, forward-only grids, readouts and e2x2. D′/E judged cells, genG, genU and GSM were not run for it.
- **Judge.** One LLM judge (gemini-2.5-flash-lite, frozen rubric), with no human raters. The keyword proxy disagrees strongly on SafeRL's style (§11).
