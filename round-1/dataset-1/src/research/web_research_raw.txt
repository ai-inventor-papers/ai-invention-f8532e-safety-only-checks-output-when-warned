=== WEB RESEARCH: 25 CANDIDATES (searched via WebSearch, 2026-09-20) ===

--- 1. tatsu-lab/alpaca ---
Paper: "Stanford Alpaca: An Instruction-following LLaMA model" (Taori et al. 2023). GitHub: tatsu-lab/stanford_alpaca. Widely cited foundational instruction-tuning dataset. 52K instructions from text-davinci-003. CC BY-NC 4.0. Downloads 115,959 -- well documented, not a red flag.

--- 2. databricks/databricks-dolly-15k ---
No academic paper; documented via Databricks blog post "Free Dolly: Introducing the World's First Truly Open Instruction-Tuned LLM" (Apr 2023). CC BY-SA 3.0. 15K human-generated instruction records. Downloads 63,201, widely used industry dataset -- provenance = blog post, not peer-reviewed paper, but well documented and highly cited in downstream work.

--- 3. JailbreakBench/JBB-Behaviors ---
Paper: "JailbreakBench: An Open Robustness Benchmark for Jailbreaking Language Models" NeurIPS 2024 Datasets & Benchmarks Track. Authors from UPenn, ETH Zurich, EPFL, Princeton. 100 misuse behaviors w/ matched benign counterparts (TWIN PAIRS). arxiv + GitHub + NeurIPS proceedings all confirm. Downloads 53,144. Strong provenance.

--- 4. carl213/advbench (mirror of walledai/AdvBench, which is GATED) ---
Underlying dataset: AdvBench from "Universal and Transferable Adversarial Attacks on Aligned Language Models" (Zou, Wang, Carlini, Nasr, Kolter, Fredrikson 2023, arXiv:2307.15043), official repo llm-attacks/llm-attacks. This specific HF mirror (carl213/advbench) is an unofficial community re-upload, 670 downloads, 0 likes, no dataset card research paper of its own -- red flag: low downloads/uncited mirror, but content is the well-known, highly-cited AdvBench (500 harmful behaviors). Original walledai/AdvBench mirror is GATED (access request required, not granted to this account).

--- 5. PKU-Alignment/BeaverTails ---
Paper: "BeaverTails: Towards Improved Safety Alignment of LLM via a Human-Preference Dataset" NeurIPS 2023 Datasets & Benchmarks (arXiv:2307.04657). 330K+ QA pairs, 14 harm categories. Downloads 15,487. Strong provenance, GitHub PKU-Alignment/beavertails.

--- 6. PKU-Alignment/PKU-SafeRLHF ---
Paper: "PKU-SafeRLHF: A Safety Alignment Preference Dataset for Llama Family Models" (arXiv:2406.15513), sibling of Safe-RLHF (ICLR 2024 Spotlight). 44.6K prompts, 265K QA pairs, 19 harm categories. Downloads 13,124. Strong provenance.

--- 7. ToxicityPrompts/wildguard-train (mirror; original allenai/wildguardmix is GATED) ---
Underlying paper: "WildGuard: Open One-Stop Moderation Tools for Safety Risks, Jailbreaks, and Refusals of LLMs" (Han, Rao, Ettinger, Jiang, Lin, Lambert, Choi, Dziri; arXiv:2406.18495). WildGuardMix = 92K labeled examples, 13 risk categories, prompt+response harm + refusal labels. This specific mirror (ToxicityPrompts/wildguard-train) has only 144 downloads, 1 like -- RED FLAG (low downloads, unofficial mirror). Official allenai/wildguardmix is GATED with access not granted to current token.

--- 8. bench-llm/or-bench ---
Paper: "OR-Bench: An Over-Refusal Benchmark for Large Language Models" (Cui, Chiang et al., arXiv:2405.20947), ICML 2025. First large-scale over-refusal benchmark: 80K prompts (or-bench-80k), ~1K hard subset (or-bench-hard-1k), 600 toxic prompts (or-bench-toxic). Downloads 8,965. Strong provenance, official GitHub justincui03/or-bench.

--- 9. nvidia/Aegis-AI-Content-Safety-Dataset-2.0 ---
Paper: "AEGIS2.0: A Diverse AI Safety Dataset and Risks Taxonomy for Alignment of LLM Guardrails" (NVIDIA). 33,416 annotated human-LLM interactions, 12 top-level hazard categories + 9 subcategories. Downloads 8,143. Strong provenance, official NVIDIA release.

--- 10. Paul/XSTest (mirror; official walledai/XSTest is GATED) ---
Underlying paper: "XSTest: A Test Suite for Identifying Exaggerated Safety Behaviours in Large Language Models" (Röttger, Kirk, Vidgen, Attanasio, Bianchi, Hovy; NAACL 2024, arXiv:2308.01263). 250 safe + 200 unsafe contrastive prompts across 10 types -- CORE TWIN-PAIR / OVER-REFUSAL DATASET for this study. Paul/XSTest mirror: 4,277 downloads, 5 likes -- moderate but has full columns (id, prompt, type, label, focus, note) matching original. Official code: github.com/paul-rottger/xstest.

--- 11. swiss-ai/harmbench (mirror; official walledai/HarmBench is GATED) ---
Underlying paper: "HarmBench: A Standardized Evaluation Framework for Automated Red Teaming and Robust Refusal" (Mazeika et al., arXiv:2402.04249). Standardized 400+ behaviors across DirectRequest/HumanJailbreaks/contextual/multimodal. This mirror (swiss-ai org, likely ETH/EPFL Swiss AI Initiative) 847 downloads, 2 likes. Official GitHub centerforaisafety/HarmBench.

--- 12. ai-safety-institute/AgentHarm ---
Paper: "AgentHarm: A Benchmark for Measuring Harmfulness of LLM Agents" (Andriushchenko, Souly et al., ICLR 2025, arXiv:2410.09024). Developed with UK AI Safety Institute + Gray Swan AI. 110 malicious agent tasks (440 w/ augmentations), 11 harm categories. Downloads 6,623. Strong provenance, official org account.

--- 13. deepset/prompt-injections ---
No standalone academic paper found; documented via deepset's Haystack blog "How to Prevent Prompt Injections: An Incomplete Guide" and used to train deepset/deberta-v3-base-injection (99.1% holdout accuracy, though real-world accuracy only ~84% per independent test -- worth noting for evaluation caution). Downloads 9,863, 183 likes -- well used despite lacking a formal paper. Binary text/label (INJECTION vs LEGIT) format, small (n<1K).

--- 14. allenai/real-toxicity-prompts ---
Paper: "RealToxicityPrompts: Evaluating Neural Toxic Degeneration in Language Models" (Gehman, Gururangan, Sap, Choi, Smith; Findings of EMNLP 2020, arXiv:2009.11462). 100K prompts from OpenWebText w/ Perspective API toxicity scores. Downloads 20,834. Strong provenance, canonical toxicity dataset.

--- 15. AlignmentResearch/StrongREJECT (mirror; official walledai/StrongREJECT is GATED) ---
Underlying paper: "A StrongREJECT for Empty Jailbreaks" (Souly, Lu, Bowen et al., NeurIPS 2024 D&B Track, arXiv:2402.10260). Standard high-quality jailbreak-effectiveness benchmark w/ automated evaluator. AlignmentResearch mirror: 520 downloads, 1 like -- low downloads (RED FLAG) but from a known safety research org (FAR.AI-affiliated "AlignmentResearch"), and preview returned no description/no sample rows (columns not confirmed in this run) -- needs re-check before use.

--- 16. LibrAI/do-not-answer ---
Paper: "Do-Not-Answer: A Dataset for Evaluating Safeguards in LLMs" (Wang, Li, Han, Nakov, Baldwin; Findings of EACL 2024, arXiv:2308.13387). Prompts curated to be ones responsible LLMs should refuse; includes GPT4/ChatGPT/Claude/ChatGLM2 response columns (useful for refusal/compliance response corpus, task d). Downloads 3,942. Strong provenance.

--- 17. natolambert/xstest-v2-copy ---
Same underlying paper as #10 (Röttger et al. NAACL 2024, arXiv:2308.01263). This is Nathan Lambert's (AI2/HuggingFace researcher) upload for easier research use, explicitly citing the source GitHub (paul-rottger/exaggerated-safety) and paper. Splits include gpt4/llama2new/llama2orig/mistralguard/mistralinstruct MODEL COMPLETIONS -- valuable refusal/compliance response corpus (task d) paired with the twin prompts (task a/b). Downloads 4,579, 7 likes. Known prior finding (memory): natolambert mirror LACKS the `focus` join key that walledai/Paul mirrors have -- confirmed relevant caveat for twin-pair joining.

--- 18. tdavidson/hate_speech_offensive ---
Paper: "Automated Hate Speech Detection and the Problem of Offensive Language" (Davidson, Warmsley, Macy, Weber; ICWSM 2017). ~24,783 annotated tweets (hate/offensive/neither). Downloads 2,492. Strong, classic, highly-cited provenance (official GitHub t-davidson/hate-speech-and-offensive-language).

--- 19. SillyTilly/SorryBench (mirror; official sorry-bench/sorry-bench-202503 is GATED) ---
Underlying paper: "SORRY-Bench: Systematically Evaluating Large Language Model Safety Refusal" (Xie, Qi, Zeng, Huang et al., ICLR 2025, arXiv:2406.14598). 44-category unsafe-topic taxonomy, 440 class-balanced instructions (9.2K total incl. augmentations). SillyTilly mirror: "Reup of Sorry Bench", 392 downloads, 1 like -- RED FLAG (low downloads, minimal card, unofficial reup) but content matches known ICLR 2025 benchmark structure (question_id, category, turns, prompt_style columns confirmed in preview).

--- 20. mlabonne/harmless_alpaca ---
No standalone paper; created by Maxime Labonne (known for the "abliteration" technique blog/Medium post) explicitly as a harmless-instruction counterpart to harmful instructions for use alongside harmful datasets -- directly relevant given this study's own topic includes "abliterated" models. Sourced from tatsu-lab/alpaca. Downloads 22,632, 47 likes, used to train 73 downstream models per HF metadata. Provenance: unverified as an academic paper but clear derivation and active reuse.

--- 21. yahma/alpaca-cleaned ---
GitHub: gururise/AlpacaDataCleaned - cleaned/curated version of Stanford Alpaca fixing hallucination-inducing and empty-output instructions. No separate academic paper (inherits Alpaca's Taori et al. 2023 provenance). Downloads 24,635, 888 likes -- widely used, well documented on GitHub with explicit changelog of fixes.

--- 22. CohereLabs/aya_redteaming ---
Paper: "The Multilingual Alignment Prism: Aligning Global and Local Preferences to Reduce Harm" (arXiv:2406.18682), part of Cohere For AI's Aya initiative. Human-curated harmful prompts across 8 languages (~900/language), 9 harm categories w/ global/local harm labels. Downloads 978, 36 likes. Strong provenance, official Cohere Labs org.

--- 23. walledai/TDC23-RedTeaming ---
Underlying competition: NeurIPS 2023 "Trojan Detection Challenge (LLM Edition)" Red Teaming Track (centerforaisafety/tdc2023-starter-kit), $30K prize competition targeting Llama-2-chat. This is a community mirror by "walledai" (Walled AI Labs, publishes several of these standardized safety mirrors) of the official competition data/behaviors.json. Downloads 375, 8 likes -- lowish downloads but traceable to an official NeurIPS competition. NOT gated (successfully previewed).

--- 24. PKU-Alignment/BeaverTails-Evaluation ---
Same paper as #5 (BeaverTails, NeurIPS 2023, arXiv:2307.04657). Official evaluation split: 700 prompts, 14 harm categories x 50 each. Downloads 588, 15 likes. Strong provenance (same official org, cited paper).

--- 25. ToxicityPrompts/PolyGuardMix -- REPLACED, see below ---
Paper: "PolyGuard: A Multilingual Safety Moderation Tool for 17 Languages" (Kumar, Jain, Yerukola, Jiang, Beniwal, Hartvigsen, Sap; COLM 2025, arXiv:2504.04377). Largest multilingual safety training corpus to date (1.91M samples/17 languages), built partly from human-verified machine translations of WildGuardMix. Downloads 523, 6 likes. STRUCK: verified real size via HfApi(files_metadata=True) = 2,306.31 MB -- FAR OVER the 300MB workspace limit. Swapped for its companion evaluation benchmark below.

--- 25 (REPLACEMENT). ToxicityPrompts/PolyGuardPrompts ---
Same paper as above (arXiv:2504.04377, COLM 2025). PolyGuardPrompts = the companion 29K-sample multilingual safety EVALUATION benchmark (vs. the 1.91M-sample training mix). Verified size = 52.86 MB (well under limit). Downloads 404, 5 likes -- lower downloads (RED FLAG, recent 2025 release) but same strong peer-reviewed provenance and official org as #7/#25-original. Split="test" (only split). Columns include prompt, response, prompt_harm_label, response_refusal_label, response_harm_label, language, subcategory -- useful multilingual content-moderation/toxicity benchmark (task f).
