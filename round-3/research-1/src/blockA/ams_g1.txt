### IV-F Algorithm Summary

The AMS scanning procedure:

  1. 1.

For each concept (harmful_content, injection_resistance, refusal_capability):

     1. (a)

Run 16 contrastive pairs through model, extract activations at layers 40-80% depth

     2. (b)

Select optimal layer (maximum separation)

     3. (c)

Compute direction vector vv and separation σ\sigma

  2. 2.

Tier 1: Classify based on minimum σ\sigma across concepts: PASS (>>3.5σ\sigma), WARNING (2.0-3.5σ\sigma), CRITICAL (<<2.0σ\sigma)

  3. 3.

Tier 2 (optional): Compare direction vectors against baseline via cosine similarity (>>0.8 required) and separation drift (<<20% required)




Total runtime: 96 forward passes (16 pairs ×\times 3 concepts ×\times ∼\sim2 for layer sweep), completing in 10-40 seconds on GPU.
