#### Activation-Based Security Analysis

Several concurrent works analyze LLM activations for security purposes. Arazzi et al. [10] derive activation fingerprints to distinguish censored from uncensored models, then exploit identified vulnerable layers for targeted attacks. Kawasaki et al. [11] propose Residual Stream Activation Analysis (RSAA) to detect jailbreak prompts by monitoring activation patterns between transformer layers. Chen et al. [12] identify “safety layers”—contiguous middle layers crucial for distinguishing malicious queries. These works focus on runtime defense (prompt-level); AMS addresses a complementary problem: pre-deployment model verification.
